## App Feature Suggestions

Based on this financial analysis, an ideal budget app should include:

1. **Income tracking** — Multiple sources (family transfer, DoorDash, Zelle)
2. **Fixed vs variable expense categorization** — Auto-categorize known merchants
3. **Credit card balance tracking** — Apple Card + BofA CC with APR awareness
4. **Pending payment alerts** — Flag large pending charges that haven't cleared
5. **Daily spending limit display** — Show remaining daily budget in real-time
6. **Category progress bars** — Visual indicators (green/yellow/red) for each budget category
7. **Month-over-month comparison** — February vs March side-by-side
8. **Subscription manager** — Track active/cancelled subscriptions
9. **Meal plan integration** — Track remaining dining swipes and dining dollars
10. **Savings goal tracker** — Monthly savings target with progress
11. **Debt payoff tracker** — Show path to zero balance
12. **Spending alerts** — Notify when approaching category limits
13. **Merchant frequency tracking** — Show how often you visit each restaurant/store
14. **Zelle transfer tracking** — Log money sent to friends/family separately from spending
